What is a virtual receptionist?
A virtual receptionist answers your business phone remotely so you never miss a call. Traditional services use shared pools of live agents billed per minute. Calling Matrix is the modern version: a custom-trained AI receptionist dedicated to your business that answers 24/7, books jobs into your scheduling software, and costs a flat monthly rate.
How much does a virtual receptionist cost?
Traditional live-agent virtual receptionist services run $285–$625+ per month plus per-minute or per-call overage charges that spike during busy weeks. Calling Matrix is $497–$797 per month flat with unlimited call answering — the price never changes no matter how many calls come in.
What's the difference between an AI virtual receptionist and a live answering service?
A live answering service connects callers to human agents who handle calls for dozens of businesses at once, working from scripts you write and maintain. An AI virtual receptionist is trained exclusively on your business — your pricing, service area, and emergency protocols — answers in under 2 seconds at any hour, and never puts a caller on hold or bills you overage.
Can it book appointments into my scheduling software?
Yes. Calling Matrix books jobs directly into ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, and Google Calendar in real time — with full customer details, job type, and urgency captured during the call. No double entry.
How fast can I get set up?
Most businesses are live within 48 hours. Setup is a single 30-minute onboarding call — we build and train your virtual receptionist for you, including your pricing, service area, and call routing rules.
Will callers know they're talking to an AI?
Callers get a natural, conversational experience with no hold time and instant answers about your pricing and availability. What they notice is that someone competent picked up on the first ring — listen to our real call demos and judge for yourself.
