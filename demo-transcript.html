Frequently asked questions

Are callers told they're talking to AI?

By default, no — but you can configure the AI to disclose if asked directly, or to always disclose upfront. Most of our clients prefer the natural approach: the AI sounds human, handles the conversation naturally, and callers rarely ask. If a caller asks directly, the AI will answer honestly based on your disclosure setting.

What if the caller has an unusual question?

The AI is trained on your specific business: your services, service area, pricing structure, and escalation rules. If a caller asks something it can't confidently answer, it collects contact info and flags it for a callback — rather than guessing or hanging up.

Can it handle accents and background noise?

Yes. The underlying speech model handles a wide range of accents, phone audio quality, and background noise (dogs, kids, road noise) well. You'll hear this in the demo recordings — the caller audio is unedited phone quality, not studio-recorded.

How is this different from an IVR or phone tree?

An IVR presents options ("press 1 for appointments"). This AI holds an open conversation — it listens, responds naturally, asks follow-up questions, and adapts to what the caller says. No menu. No "I didn't understand that, please try again."

What happens after the call?

Every call is logged with a full transcript and recording in your dashboard. If a job was booked, it goes straight into your calendar. If a message was taken, you get a notification. You review calls at your convenience — not in real time.
