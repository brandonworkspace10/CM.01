How it works

Inbound call to Jobber job
in under 60 seconds.

Calling Matrix handles the full intake — bookings and quote requests — and writes everything into Jobber automatically. No dispatcher needed for routine calls.

Jobs and quotes both handled

Calling Matrix identifies whether a caller wants to book or get a quote, then creates the right Jobber record. Immediate bookings get a job with a confirmed time; quote requests get a detailed quote entry for your team to follow up on.

Client records created automatically

New callers get a fresh client profile in Jobber with name, phone, address, and service history from the call. Returning clients are matched and their records updated — no duplicates, no missing data.

Residential and commercial workflows

Configure separate intake flows for residential and commercial callers. Different questions, urgency levels, and routing rules ensure every job type reaches the right team with the right context.

Emergency escalation

Urgent calls are flagged and your on-call team is notified immediately by text — even while the Jobber job is being created. No emergency waits until morning because it came in after hours.

FAQ

Questions about the
Jobber integration.

How does the Jobber integration work?
Calling Matrix connects to your Jobber account via API. When a call comes in, it captures the client's name, address, service requested, and preferred time, then creates a job or quote request directly in Jobber — with full call notes attached. New clients get a profile created automatically.
Can it handle quote requests as well as bookings?
Yes. Calling Matrix distinguishes between callers who want to book immediately and those who want a quote first. For quote requests, it captures the scope of work, creates a quote request in Jobber with all the details, and notifies your team to follow up — so no lead falls through the cracks.
Does it work for both residential and commercial Jobber accounts?
Yes. Calling Matrix supports both residential and commercial Jobber workflows. During onboarding, we configure separate intake flows for each — different questions, urgency levels, and routing rules — so the right jobs go to the right team.
What plan includes Jobber sync?
Jobber integration is included in all Calling Matrix plans at no additional cost. During your 30-minute onboarding call, we connect your Jobber account, configure your service types, and run a test booking before you go live.
How long does setup take?
Most Jobber users are live in 48 hours. We start with a 30-minute onboarding call to connect your account, configure your services and territory, then build and test your AI receptionist. Same-week go-live is standard.
