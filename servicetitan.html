How it works

From inbound call to ServiceTitan job
in under 60 seconds.

Calling Matrix handles the entire intake process and writes the result directly into ServiceTitan — no CSR required for routine bookings.

Call answered in under 2 seconds

Every inbound call is picked up instantly, day or night. The AI greets callers using your company name, captures their issue, and begins building the ServiceTitan record in real time.

Customer matched or created

Returning customers are matched by phone number and their records updated. New callers get a fresh contact entry in ServiceTitan with all intake details captured during the call.

Job booked into live schedule

Calling Matrix reads your ServiceTitan schedule in real time, offers available slots to the caller, and creates the job with the confirmed time — no double-booking, no back-and-forth.

Full call data synced

Job type, urgency level, customer notes, preferred technician, and a full call summary are all written to the ServiceTitan job record. Your dispatcher has everything they need before they even open the job.

FAQ

Questions about the
ServiceTitan integration.

Does it work with all ServiceTitan business units?
Yes. Calling Matrix supports multi-business-unit ServiceTitan accounts. During onboarding, we configure which business unit receives bookings from which call types, so every job lands in the right place automatically.
How does it book jobs in ServiceTitan?
Calling Matrix reads your live ServiceTitan schedule, identifies open slots that match the caller's requested service and location, and creates the job directly in ServiceTitan. The customer record is created or matched, the job type is assigned, and your dispatcher sees it immediately.
Does it update existing customer records in ServiceTitan?
Yes. When a returning customer calls, Calling Matrix matches them by phone number in ServiceTitan. It updates contact details if they've changed and appends call notes to the existing record — no duplicate entries, no manual cleanup.
What call data syncs to ServiceTitan?
Every job created by Calling Matrix includes the customer's name, phone, address, service type requested, preferred appointment time, urgency level, and a full summary of the call. Call recordings are also linked so your team has full context.
How long does setup take?
Most ServiceTitan users are live in 48 hours. We start with a 30-minute onboarding call to connect your ServiceTitan account, configure your job types, business units, and territories, then build and test your AI receptionist. Same-week go-live is standard.
