How routing works

Three tiers. Your rules.
Zero missed emergencies.

During onboarding we configure exactly what gets your tech out of bed and what waits until 8 AM. Then every call gets triaged in real time.

Tier 1 — True emergency

Burst pipe, no heat in a freeze, electrical hazard, active leak. Your on-call tech gets an immediate call or text with the customer's name, address, and issue — while the caller is still on the line.

Tier 2 — Urgent, books next slot

Needs attention soon but not at 2 AM. The AI books the first available slot tomorrow directly into ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber — customer confirmed, job on the board before you wake up.

Tier 3 — Routine, captured & confirmed

Quotes, reschedules, general questions. Full details captured, appointment booked or callback queued, confirmation sent to the customer — nothing for your team to chase in the morning.

Morning report, every day

Wake up to a clean summary: every call, every booking, every dispatch, with recordings and transcripts. You see exactly what happened overnight — no mystery messages.

FAQ

After-hours answering,
asked and answered.

How does it handle real emergencies?
Calling Matrix uses three-tier routing configured to your rules. True emergencies — burst pipes, no heat in winter, electrical hazards — trigger an immediate call or text to your on-call technician with the customer's name, address, and issue. Non-urgent calls get booked into your next available slot. Everything else gets captured and confirmed for morning.
What counts as an emergency vs. a next-day call?
You decide. During onboarding we configure your emergency thresholds — what gets your tech out of bed and what waits until 8 AM. The AI triages every call against your rules and errs on the side you choose.
How much does an after-hours answering service cost?
Traditional after-hours services bill per call or per minute — which gets expensive exactly when you're busiest. Calling Matrix is $497–$797 per month flat with unlimited calls, 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays.
Can it cover just nights and weekends, or does it replace my daytime staff?
Either. Many companies forward their phones only after closing time and keep their office staff answering during the day. Others route everything through Calling Matrix 24/7. Call forwarding rules are set by you and can change anytime.
What does my on-call tech actually receive?
A text or call (your choice) with the customer's name, phone number, address, and a summary of the issue — sent while the caller is still on the line. Recordings and transcripts are available for every call.
How fast can coverage go live?
Most companies are live within 48 hours of a 30-minute onboarding call. Tonight's missed calls don't have to happen next week.
