About Calling Matrix

Built for the owner
who can't afford to miss a call.

Calling Matrix is a 24/7 AI receptionist for home service businesses — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, and cleaning. We answer every call, route every emergency, and book every job automatically.

The story

Home service businesses run on calls. Every unanswered ring is a job that goes to the next plumber in the list, the next HVAC company on Google, the next contractor willing to pick up. The average missed call in this industry is worth $400–$650. Most owners miss dozens every week.

The problem isn't that owners don't care — it's that they're on a job, under a sink, on a roof, or running a crew. A phone call at 2 AM during a burst pipe emergency doesn't wait for a callback. The homeowner dials the next number.

"The contractor who answers first wins the job. Full stop."

Calling Matrix was built to be that answer. A custom-trained AI receptionist that sounds like your dispatcher, knows your services and pricing, routes emergencies to your on-call tech, and books jobs straight into your calendar — around the clock, without you lifting a finger.

We're not a call center. We're not an IVR that frustrates callers into hanging up. We build a voice that represents your business — one that handles the Friday night burst pipe the same way your best employee would, every single time.

The founder

B

Brandon Ortiz

Founder, Calling Matrix

Brandon built Calling Matrix after watching home service businesses — the kind that keep cities running — lose revenue every single day to a problem that was completely solvable. Not a staffing problem. Not a technology problem. A timing problem: the phone rings when no one can answer it.

Calling Matrix operates under the DBA Calling Matrix and serves home service businesses across the United States. The focus is narrow by design: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, and cleaning — the five trades where after-hours and emergency call handling makes the biggest revenue difference.
