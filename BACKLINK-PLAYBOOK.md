# Calling Matrix — Backlink Playbook

Goal: go from DR 0 to DR 15–25 in 90 days with links that also send qualified
traffic and feed AI-assistant citations. Work top to bottom — Tier 1 is free and
mostly a matter of filling out forms.

Cadence: 30–60 minutes, 2× per week. Track every submission in a simple list
(date, site, status, live URL). Claude can draft any outreach email below as a
Gmail draft on request.

---

## Tier 1 — Foundation (week 1, all free, do these first)

These are the profiles Google and AI assistants check to verify you're a real
company. Use the EXACT same name, description, and URL everywhere — copy the
canonical sentence from `.agents/product-marketing.md`.

| # | Where | Why | Action |
|---|-------|-----|--------|
| 1 | Google Business Profile | Single biggest local visibility lever; feeds Google AND Gemini | Create/claim, category "Answering service", add real photos |
| 2 | G2.com | Ranks for "AI receptionist" terms itself; LLMs cite G2 constantly | Create vendor profile, then push every happy client for a review |
| 3 | Capterra + GetApp + Software Advice | One Gartner network submission covers all three | List under "Answering Service Software" |
| 4 | Product Hunt | DR 90 link + launch-day traffic spike | Plan a proper launch (Tuesday–Thursday) |
| 5 | Crunchbase | Standard company-verification citation for LLMs | Free company profile |
| 6 | LinkedIn company page | Cited entity source; also where your buyers are | Complete profile, link to site |
| 7 | Bing Places + Apple Business Connect | Copilot and Siri/Apple Maps visibility | Free listings |
| 8 | TrustRadius, Clutch | Secondary review sites that rank for "best answering service" | Basic profiles |

## Tier 2 — Industry-specific (weeks 2–4, highest-value links)

**The killer move: integration partner directories.** You already have dedicated
pages for ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and Jobber. Each of those companies runs
a partner/integration marketplace that links back to listed partners. Those are
high-DR, perfectly relevant links your competitors mostly don't have:

1. **Jobber App Marketplace** — apply at developer.getjobber.com
2. **Housecall Pro integrations/partner directory** — partner application
3. **ServiceTitan marketplace** — longer approval cycle, start now
4. **Zapier app directory** — you already advertise Zapier support; a published
   Zapier integration gets a permanent DR 93 listing page
5. **Workiz & FieldEdge partner pages** — same play

Also in this tier:
- Trade association resource/vendor directories: ACCA (HVAC), PHCC (plumbing),
  NECA (electrical), NRCA (roofing), ISSA (cleaning). Some require membership —
  the local chapters are cheaper and link too.
- Local chamber of commerce (cheap, legitimate local citation).

## Tier 3 — Content-driven links (ongoing)

1. **Pitch the $287 stat.** `/blog/missed-call-value` is your linkable asset.
   Pitch it as a data story to trade publications: ACHR News, Contractor
   Magazine, Plumber Magazine, CleanLink, Roofing Contractor. Subject line:
   "Data: the average missed call costs an HVAC company $287."
2. **Guest spots on trade podcasts.** Service Business Mastery, HVAC School,
   The Build Show, ToolBox for the Trades (ServiceTitan's own podcast — doubles
   as partner relationship). Pitch the founder story + missed-call math, not
   the product.
3. **Source-request platforms.** Qwoted / Featured / Help a B2B Writer — answer
   2–3 journalist requests per week about AI for small business, customer
   service automation, or the trades. Each placement is a DR 60+ editorial link.
4. **Reddit & communities (AI visibility, not links).** r/HVAC, r/Plumbing,
   r/smallbusiness threads about missed calls get scraped into LLM training and
   cited by Perplexity. Be a helpful contractor-tech person, not an advertiser:
   answer questions honestly, mention the product only when directly relevant.

## What NOT to do

- No purchased link packages, no Fiverr "DR 50 guest posts" — at DR 0 a spam
  penalty is fatal.
- No mass directory-submission tools. 20 hand-picked listings beat 500 junk ones.
- Don't put different company descriptions on different sites — entity
  consistency is the whole game for AI citations.

## 90-day expectation

- Weeks 1–2: ~15 foundation citations live
- Weeks 3–6: 3–5 marketplace/partner links live, first review batch on G2
- Weeks 7–12: 2–3 editorial links from pitches/podcasts
- Result: DR ~15–25, indexed brand entity, first AI-assistant citations
  (test monthly: ask ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity "best AI receptionist for HVAC")
