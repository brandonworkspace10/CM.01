01 / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Calling Matrix, Inc. ("Calling Matrix," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at callingmatrix.com. Short version: We collect only what you give us through our contact form. We use it to respond to your inquiry. We don't sell it.

02 / Data collection

Information we collect

Information you provide directly

When you submit our contact form, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Business name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business type (e.g., HVAC, plumbing)
  • Estimated monthly call volume
  • Any message or notes you include

This information is submitted voluntarily. You are not required to provide it to browse the site.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we and our third-party analytics providers automatically collect certain technical data, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and time spent on pages. This data is used in aggregate and is not linked to your identity.

03 / Data use

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and schedule consultations
  • Send you a confirmation email upon form submission
  • Follow up about Calling Matrix products and services
  • Understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

04 / Third parties

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data on our behalf. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

Resend

We use Resend to deliver transactional emails. When you submit our contact form, your name, email address, and form contents are transmitted to Resend's servers to send you a confirmation email and to deliver your inquiry to our team. Resend's privacy policy is available at resend.com.

Vercel Analytics

Our website is hosted on Vercel, which provides anonymous, aggregated analytics about site usage. Vercel Analytics does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information. Vercel's privacy policy is available at vercel.com.

Ahrefs

We use Ahrefs web analytics to understand traffic sources and search performance. Ahrefs collects anonymous usage data including pages visited and referral sources. Ahrefs' privacy policy is available at ahrefs.com.

Calendly

If you choose to book a consultation through our Calendly link, Calendly collects scheduling information including your name, email address, and selected time slot. That data is governed by Calendly's privacy policy, available at calendly.com.

05 / Security

Data security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the personal information we collect, consistent with the requirements of the New York SHIELD Act. These measures include rate limiting on form submissions, transport encryption (HTTPS), and access controls limiting who can view submitted contact data.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as necessary to manage our business relationship with you or as required by law. If you request deletion of your information, we will remove it from our active records within 30 days, subject to any legal retention requirements.

Children's privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it promptly.
