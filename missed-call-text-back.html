FAQ

Missed call text back questions,
answered straight.

What is missed call text back?
A system that automatically sends a text message to anyone whose call you miss, within seconds. Instead of hitting voicemail and calling your competitor, the customer gets an instant response and a way to book. Calling Matrix goes further than basic tools: our AI continues the text conversation, qualifies the job, and books the appointment.
How fast is the text back?
Under 10 seconds from the missed call. Speed is the whole point — the customer is still holding their phone, still thinking about their problem, and hasn't searched for anyone else yet.
Can it actually book jobs, or just reply?
It books. The AI asks the right questions — job type, location, urgency — answers questions about your services, and schedules directly into ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, or Google Calendar.
What happens with emergencies?
Texts that indicate a true emergency — burst pipe, no heat, sparking panel — get flagged and routed to your on-call tech immediately by call or text, following the rules you set at onboarding.
How is this different from the AI receptionist?
The AI receptionist answers the call itself, so it never gets missed. Missed call text back is the lighter option: your phones keep working exactly as they do now, and the AI only steps in when a call slips through. Many customers start here and upgrade.
Is the texting compliant?
Yes. We register your number for A2P 10DLC (the carrier requirement for business texting), only text people who called you first, and honor opt-outs instantly.
