FAQ

Questions cleaning business owners
ask us every day.

Can it book recurring weekly or biweekly appointments?
Yes. It books single sessions and recurring schedules, confirms the recurrence with the client, and syncs everything to your calendar. Clients can also call back to change their frequency and it handles that automatically.
What if a client needs to reschedule?
Calling Matrix handles reschedule requests, finds the next available slot based on your calendar, confirms the new time with the client, and updates your scheduling software — without any involvement from your team.
Does it follow up on quote requests?
Yes. If a caller requests a quote but doesn't book, automated follow-up messages go out at 24, 48, and 72 hours after the initial call — turning cold leads into confirmed appointments.
Can it screen for our service area?
Yes. It asks for the client's zip code and confirms whether it's within your service territory before proceeding with intake — so you never send a crew to an out-of-range address.
How does it handle questions about our pricing?
We load your pricing during onboarding — rates by home size, cleaning type, and frequency — so callers get accurate quotes immediately without waiting for a callback.
