Questions

Quick answers before your consultation

How long does setup actually take?
Most HVAC shops are live in 48 hours. Day 1 is your 30-minute onboarding call. Day 2 your number is active and your calendar is synced. That's it — you don't touch anything after that.
Will it work with Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan?
Yes — direct, two-way integration with Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Jobber, FieldEdge, and Google Calendar. Bookings drop into your dispatch board in real time.
What if I already have a business phone number?
We port it over so your customers keep dialing the same number. If you'd prefer a fresh local number we provision one for you. Either way we handle the whole process — typically takes 1–3 business days.
Does it handle Spanish-speaking customers?
Yes — fully bilingual. It detects the caller's language and responds naturally in English or Spanish. No scripts, no delays, no awkward switching mid-call.
